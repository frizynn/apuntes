# Lecture 1: BATNA and reservation point

This lecture answers one question: when should you say yes, and when should you walk away?

## Your alternative: the BATNA

Your **BATNA** (Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement) is what you will do if there is no deal. It is not what you hope to get: it is your real plan B.

It matters because it sets your power. With a good alternative you can ask for more and walk away without fear. If you are looking for a job and already hold another offer, you negotiate salary calmly.

## The reservation point

The **reservation point** is the worst deal you would still accept. It comes from your BATNA: if your other offer pays 3,000, your reservation point is close to 3,000.

Do not reveal it. If the other side knows it, they will offer exactly that.

## Key takeaways

- The BATNA is your alternative without a deal; the reservation point is the worst deal you accept.
- The reservation point comes from the BATNA.
- A strong BATNA gives power; never reveal your reservation point.
